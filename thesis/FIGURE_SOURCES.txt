SOURCE AND ATTRIBUTION OF THE ORIGINAL TRANSFORMER FIGURE

Chapter 2 of the thesis reproduces the original Figure 1 of the paper:
Ashish Vaswani et al., "Attention Is All You Need", NeurIPS 2017.
arXiv:1706.03762, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1706.03762
Source of the file: the image embedded in page 3 of the paper PDF
(1520 x 2239 pixels), extracted without redrawing it.
For reproduction on a white background, only the transparency mask
included in the original PDF was applied.

On the first page of the paper Google grants permission to reproduce
its figures and tables in scholarly or journalistic works, provided
proper attribution is given. The corresponding citation is
\cite{vaswani2017attention}.

The diagram shows the ORIGINAL 2017 encoder-decoder Transformer;
it is not the technical diagram of STERA 30M. STERA 30M is decoder-only
and its own diagram is in Chapter 7. The paper figure is not used to
attribute a separate encoder or a cross-attention layer to STERA.

The other figures of the thesis describe RowMask23 and the model studied.
